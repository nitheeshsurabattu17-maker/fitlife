Standard monthly memberships start at approximately ₹1,500/month. We also provide cost-effective multi-month bundles (3 months, 6 months, and 12 months) that significantly reduce the per-month rate, as well as customized 1-on-1 personal training packages with Coaches Chakri and Jani.

Fitlife Gym is located on the 3rd Floor of the Phoenix Padmavathi Building on Koritepadu Main Road, directly opposite the LVR Club and above the Puma Showroom in Lakshmipuram, Guntur. Secure parking is readily available around the building.
