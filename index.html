ABOUT FITLIFE

MORE THAN A GYM.
IT'S A LIFESTYLE.

Fitlife Gym is Guntur's benchmark for structured, results-driven fitness. Located on the 3rd Floor of the landmark Phoenix Padmavathi Building in Lakshmipuram, we have built a space where discipline meets modern athletic science.

Whether you are lifting your first barbell, aiming for metabolic fat loss, or conditioning for peak athletic performance, Fitlife provides a high-energy environment supported by seasoned coaches Chakri and Jani, Olympic free weights, and dedicated recovery spaces.

Spacious & Ventilated Training Arena

Generous machine spacing, air-conditioning, and acoustic motivation ensure you train at peak focus without feeling cramped.

Attentive Coaching by Chakri & Jani

Direct movement biomechanics guidance, posture correction, and tailored Indian diet charts calculated for your metabolism.

Complete High-Performance Ecosystem

From Olympic power squat cages and commercial cardio decks to functional battle ropes, boxing gear, and a thermal dry sauna.
