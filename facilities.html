TRAINING & FACILITIES

Explore each verified training zone on our 3rd-floor facility at Phoenix Padmavathi Building. Engineered to provide the exact tools you need for strength, fat loss, athletic conditioning, and recovery.

ZONE 01 • STRENGTH

OLYMPIC STRENGTH & FREE WEIGHTS

The core of Fitlife Gym. Built for lifters who prioritize fundamental compound movements—squatting, bench pressing, deadlifting, and overhead pressing. Our heavy-duty racks and Olympic-calibrated bars ensure stability and safety under heavy loads.

Dumbbells from 2.5 kg to 40+ kg
Heavy Power Squat Cages
Incline, Flat & Decline Benches
Cable Crossover & Lat Machines

CARDIO & ENDURANCE DECK

Whether warming up prior to lifting or executing intense cardiovascular intervals for fat loss, our cardio deck is lined with commercial-grade treadmills, stationary bicycles, and cross-trainers featuring shock-absorbent decks to protect your knees.

High-Incline Motorized Treadmills
Low-Impact Cross Trainers
Spin & Stationary Cycles
Heart Rate & Calorie Monitors

1-ON-1 PERSONAL TRAINING & DIET

Fitlife Gym's hallmark service. Guided by senior coaches Chakri and Jani, you receive a full anatomical movement assessment, tailored weekly progression goals, and an individualized Indian dietary plan calibrated for steady fat reduction or clean lean muscle synthesis.

Daily Form & Rep Supervision
Customized Indian Diet Charts
Bi-weekly Measurements & Body Fat

FUNCTIONAL & CROSS-TRAINING

Break away from fixed machine planes and develop rotational torque, grip endurance, and lung power. Our functional area incorporates heavy dual battle ropes, competition cast-iron kettlebells, plyometric jumping boxes, and medicine slam balls.

Heavy-Duty Double Battle Ropes
Kettlebells & Slam Balls
Plyometric Jump Boxes
Core & Kinetic Rotational Rigs
